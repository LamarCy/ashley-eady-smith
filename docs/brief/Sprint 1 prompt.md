## Opening ident (first thing a visitor sees)
Her handwritten signature is the brand mark and the opening. Files in this folder: `signature.png` (1876x855 scan) and `signature.svg` (a single filled path traced from it, `fill-rule="evenodd"`, viewBox 0 0 1876 855). Copy both to `public/brand/`.
- Build `<SignatureIdent>` as a fixed full-viewport overlay that renders only on `/` and only when `sessionStorage.aesIdentSeen` is unset. The hero beneath renders immediately; the ident must never block LCP or hide content from crawlers.
- Beat 0 (0 to 0.3s): paper ground `#f4f2ed` with the cprice.studio gateway fog: 3 or 4 absolutely positioned spans, `border-radius:50%`, `background:radial-gradient(ellipse at 50% 50%,#ffffffe6,#ffffff9e 42%,#fff0 72%)`, `filter:blur(44px)`, drifting with `@keyframes drift-a{0%,to{transform:translateZ(0)}50%{transform:translate3d(6%,-2%,0)}}` over 24 to 36s.
- Beat 1 (0.3 to 2.5s): the signature writes itself. Implement as an inline SVG: the filled signature `<path>` in ink `#111110` is revealed through `<mask>` containing ONE thick stroked path (stroke `#fff`, `stroke-width` about 110 in viewBox units, `stroke-linecap:round`, `stroke-linejoin:round`, `fill:none`) whose geometry follows the pen's own order through the signature from the left flourish to the final two strokes on the right. Author that centerline by hand: open `signature.png`, trace a polyline of 50 to 80 points along the middle of the stroke in writing order (it is one connected stroke with a few lifts; for lifts, continue the path through the gap so the mask still covers it), smooth it with Catmull-Rom to cubic Béziers, and verify by rendering the mask path semi-transparent over the signature and screenshotting it; iterate until the mask covers every part of the ink. Animate `stroke-dasharray`/`stroke-dashoffset` from the path's `getTotalLength()` to 0 over 2.2s with `cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1)`. If after a reasonable effort the centerline is not covering well, fall back to a soft-edged left-to-right wipe: a `<linearGradient>` mask with a 12% feathered edge translated across the signature over 1.8s. Either way it must look like handwriting appearing, not a slideshow.
- Beat 2 (2.5 to 2.9s): hold; a faint Grenadine Pink `#f48067` radial glow at 18% opacity blooms behind the ink and fades.
- Beat 3 (2.9 to 3.4s): FLIP transition. Measure the header's signature slot, then animate the big signature's transform (translate + scale) into that slot (about 140px wide) while the fog and overlay fade to 0 and the hero fades up (`opacity` 0 to 1, `translateY` 12px to 0, 600ms). Then unmount the overlay and set `sessionStorage.aesIdentSeen = "1"`.
- Skip: any click, tap or keypress jumps to Beat 3. A "Skip" label in DM Mono caps, bottom-right, ink at 60% opacity.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`: no draw-on and no FLIP; the signature fades in over 600ms, holds 400ms, fades out, hero appears. Total under 1.5s.
- The signature replaces any monogram: it is the header mark (links to `/`), the footer mark, the fixed bottom-right medallion (signature in ink on a 54px paper disc with the C.PRICE medallion's shadow, like the C.P medallion on cprice.studio), the favicon (a simplified crop of the big loop, generated as SVG + PNG), and the Open Graph image (signature over the sky photo).

## Design system (inherit from cprice.studio, my other site)
- Stack: Astro 5, zero JS frameworks, vanilla CSS with custom properties, @fontsource/eb-garamond (400, 500, italic) and @fontsource/dm-mono (400, 500).
- Ground: paper #f4f2ed, off-white #f9f9f7, ink #111110, ink-mid #444440, ink-light #888882, rule #d6d3cc.
- Palette: Sanzo Wada, Dictionary of Color Combinations no. 193. Naples Yellow #fbe6a0 (washes, highlights, hover fills, never text), Grenadine Pink #f48067 (primary accent for fills: pull-quote rule, ident glow, hover highlights; never text, it is 2.3:1 on paper), Light Porcelain Green #00908a (italic headline word, callout rule, focus rings) with a deepened shade #00726d for small text such as links and mono labels (5.2:1 on paper). Expose all as CSS variables in `src/styles/tokens.css`.
- Type: EB Garamond for headlines and body (clamp 2.6rem to 5rem for h1, italic for the emphasized word, which is set in Porcelain Green). DM Mono uppercase with 0.14em tracking for eyebrows, metadata, prices, labels. Hairline rules between list rows. Generous whitespace; the above-the-fold area is mostly paper and Naples Yellow with the portrait as the only dark mass.
- Buttons / CTAs: every call to action is a STICKY NOTE copied from the cprice.studio gateway, not a conventional button. Copy `notes/` to `public/notes/`. Build a `<NoteCTA>` Astro component with this exact recipe.
